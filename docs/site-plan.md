# Narrcorp website plan

Status: proposed site and content scope, 8 November 2026.

## Purpose

Help business decision makers identify a technology need, understand how Narrcorp could support it, and email a project brief to hr@narrcorp.com.

Lead with business problems and useful next steps. Proposed services require business confirmation before the finished site is published.

## Page structure

| Section | Content | Visitor action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header | Narrcorp wordmark; About, Services and Why Narrcorp anchors; Let's talk button | Navigate or email |
| Hero | One clear business headline, supporting paragraph and original visual | Start a conversation or view services |
| About | Business-first proposition and engagement approach | Understand the offering |
| Services | Eight concise service groups describing useful work | Identify a relevant need |
| Why Narrcorp | Clear scope, usable solutions, operational planning and flexible expertise | Assess the approach |
| Testimonials | Real, permissioned customer quotations with accurate attribution | Assess credible proof |
| CTA | Direct invitation and guidance on what to include in an email | Email hr@narrcorp.com |
| Footer | Narrcorp name, copyright and email as the only contact information | Email |

If testimonials are unavailable, omit the section. Do not publish placeholders, invented names, ratings, logos, metrics or reviews. The remaining seven sections still form the complete landing page.

## Proposed service groups

1. Technology strategy and roadmaps: discovery, system reviews, delivery priorities and investment planning.
2. Workflow automation: repetitive-task reduction, approvals, document workflows and integrations.
3. Digital products and customer experiences: websites, portals, internal tools, UX and application engineering.
4. Connected business systems: APIs, application integration, CRM and ERP workflows, including SAP and Salesforce where supported.
5. Data, analytics and applied AI: pipelines, reporting, knowledge search and scoped AI use cases.
6. Cloud platforms and delivery operations: migration planning, infrastructure automation, CI/CD and observability.
7. Quality, security and resilience: testing, access reviews, recovery planning and application reliability.
8. Specialist teams and delivery support: staff augmentation, direct hire and dedicated engineering teams where offered.

Confirm actual delivery coverage, engagement models and boundaries before publication. Do not claim proprietary platforms, guaranteed outcomes, round-the-clock support or certifications without evidence.

## Content and visual principles

Use plain English and concrete service descriptions. Avoid unsupported scale, rankings or results. Do not add an industry list, blog, careers portal or additional pages to this scope.

Recommended direction: warm off-white background, ink typography, restrained indigo and coral accents, large editorial headings, spacious service rows and one original hero composition. This visual direction remains a proposal for review.

Use original or licensed media. Illustrative people must not be represented as actual employees, clients or reviewers. Keep meaningful text in HTML rather than embedding it in images.

## Interaction and delivery

Contact buttons use mailto:hr@narrcorp.com. This opens the visitor's configured email application; it does not submit a form or confirm delivery.

Build static HTML, CSS and minimal JavaScript. Use progressive enhancement for mobile navigation, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic headings, reduced-motion support and responsive images. Optional video needs a still-image fallback and must not obscure the headline or CTA.

Verify phone, tablet and desktop layouts, keyboard navigation, anchors, email links, image sizing and publishing checks before release. Hostinger is the planned hosting provider. The purchased plan, domain ownership and mailbox operation remain operational checks before launch. See the development guide for the proposed stack and release process.
