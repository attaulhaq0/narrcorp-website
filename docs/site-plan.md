# Narrcorp website plan

Status: implemented static release, 9 November 2026. Business copy approval and live hosting checks remain part of launch.

## Purpose

Help business decision makers identify a technology need, understand how Narrcorp could support it, and email a project brief to hr@narrcorp.com.

Lead with business problems and useful next steps. Proposed services require business confirmation before the finished site is published.

## Page structure

| Section              | Content                                                                       | Visitor action                        |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Header               | Announcement; wordmark; centered section anchors; expertise and email buttons | Navigate or email                     |
| Hero                 | One clear business headline, supporting paragraph and original visual         | Start a conversation or view services |

Testimonials remain omitted until genuine, approved quotations are supplied. Platform icons provide technical context without claiming customer relationships or partnerships. The overview counts describe the page's eight services, three staffing arrangements and four delivery principles, rather than historical performance.

## Proposed service groups

1. Technology strategy and roadmaps: discovery, system reviews, delivery priorities and investment planning.
2. Workflow automation: repetitive-task reduction, approvals, document workflows and integrations.
3. Digital products and customer experiences: websites, portals, internal tools, UX and application engineering.
4. Connected business systems: APIs, application integration, CRM and ERP workflows, including SAP and Salesforce where supported.
5. Data, analytics and applied AI: pipelines, reporting, knowledge search and scoped AI use cases.
6. Cloud platforms and delivery operations: migration planning, infrastructure automation, CI/CD and observability.
7. Quality, security and resilience: testing, access reviews, recovery planning and application reliability.
8. Specialist teams and delivery support: staff augmentation, direct hire and dedicated engineering teams where offered.

Confirm actual delivery coverage, engagement models and boundaries before publication. Do not claim proprietary platforms, guaranteed outcomes, round-the-clock support or certifications without evidence.

## Content and visual principles

Use plain English and concrete service descriptions. Avoid unsupported scale, rankings or results. Industry scenarios and guidance stay on this landing page; they are not customer case studies. No careers portal, forms or additional service routes are included.

Visual direction: white and mint backgrounds, forest-green accents and deep ink, large serif headings, rounded media cards and generous spacing. The hero uses a responsive sequence with portrait photography, moving callouts and a workflow demonstration. Services have three visual highlights followed by an expandable directory covering all eight offerings. Cards share a sticky position on desktop and stack normally on phones and tablets. One skill ribbon runs along the bottom of the talent visual. Additional motion includes platform marquees, scroll highlights, ecosystem reveals and vertically moving engagement cards. Device reduced-motion preferences stop decorative motion and remove sticky layering.

Use original or licensed media. Illustrative people must not be represented as actual employees, clients or reviewers. Keep meaningful text in HTML rather than embedding it in images.

## Interaction and delivery

The header and closing section's email buttons use mailto:hr@narrcorp.com. Discussion buttons lead to the closing contact section. Email links open the visitor's configured email application; they do not submit a form or confirm delivery.

Build with Astro, TypeScript and regular CSS using static output. Astro produces HTML, CSS, responsive images and minimal JavaScript for hosting. Use progressive enhancement for mobile navigation, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic headings, reduced-motion support and responsive images. Optional video needs a still-image fallback and must not obscure the headline or CTA.

Verify phone, tablet and desktop layouts, keyboard navigation, anchors, email links, image sizing and publishing checks before release. Hostinger is the planned hosting provider. The purchased plan, domain ownership and mailbox operation remain operational checks before launch. See the development guide for the proposed stack and release process.
